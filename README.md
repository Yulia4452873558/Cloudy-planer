# ☁️ Cloudy

*Cloudy is a delicate pastel glider that makes you want to plan.** 💜

Together with a Cloud, you will make your day, you will not forget about yourself and you will enjoy every little victory.

## 📝 Today
- Tasks and habits for every day.
- Repetition of habits on selected days of the week.
- 12 ready-made categories and the ability to create your own with unique colors and icons.
- Weekly review with marks of completed days in the form of stars.
- Reminders at any convenient time.
- Swipe removal of cards and the ability to restore them with a single button.

## , Calendar
- Birthdays, holidays and important dates.
- Countdown to special events.

## , Schedule
- Time planning of cases.
- Automatic sorting of tasks by the hour.

## 💗 Health
- Menstrual cycle calendar with forecast.
- Water tracker.
- Mood tracking.
- Sleep accounting.

## ⏱ Focus
- Timer for work, study, reading, sports and favorite activities.
- Notifications with "Pause" and "Cancel" buttons.
- The timer keeps counting down even if you close the app.
- Weekly goals and time schedule.
- Calm music that plays even when the screen is turned off.

## 🏆 Awards
- A series of completed days.
- Personal bests.
- Achievement badges.

## ✨ Features
- 🌤️ Light and dark themes.
- 🔒 Without registration.
- All data is stored locally on the phone.

## 💜 Cloudy Philosophy

Cloudy is not about perfect productivity, but about self—care, calm planning and small steps towards big goals.

You don't have to keep up with everything. It is important to find time for yourself, notice your successes and enjoy every day.

*Cloudy - plan with tenderness! 💜**
